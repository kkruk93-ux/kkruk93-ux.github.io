3D brain mesh
===============
Title:  3D Reference Organ for Brain, Male, v1.3
Authors: Kristen Browne; Heidi Schlehlein (2023)
Publisher: Human Reference Atlas / HuBMAP
DOI: https://doi.org/10.48539/HBM929.XKCL.339
NIH 3D: https://3d.nih.gov/entries/20960
Library: https://humanatlas.io/3d-reference-library
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
         https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

This reference organ was created using data from the
Allen Human Reference Atlas – 3D, 2020 (Ding et al., 2016).
The anatomical structures were mirrored to a whole brain and
resized to the Visible Human Male.

Adaptations by Rovi & Shadow Studio (2026):
  - kept outer cortex, cerebellum and brainstem meshes
  - merged by lobe / hemisphere
  - reduced polygon count for mobile
  - generated stylized (illustrative, not measured) DTI-like
    white-matter streamlines for visualization

No endorsement by the authors, Human Reference Atlas, HuBMAP,
the Allen Institute or NIH is implied.

White-matter fibers in this app are procedural visualization
aids, not a tractography atlas and not derived from a measured
DTI dataset.
